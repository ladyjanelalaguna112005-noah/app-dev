**All of Us Are Dead** is a South Korean teen horror series set in a high school where a deadly virus outbreak turns students into zombies. Trapped inside the school with survival and friendship on the line, a small group of classmates must fight, hide, and make impossible choices to stay alive.

> "When the world outside collapses, the school becomes its own terrifying universe."

**Main Characters:**
- **Jong-su (Lee Cheong-san)** — A brave and resourceful student who tries to protect his friends.  
- **On-jo (Choi Nam-ra)** — Jong-su’s childhood friend, compassionate and determined.  
- **Nam-ra (Yoon Gwi-nam)** — Strong-willed and fiercely loyal to her friends.  
- **Su-hyeok (Yoon Gwi-nam)** — A calm presence who balances leadership with survival instincts.  
- **Lee Su-hyun** — A student whose actions highlight how fear and hope shape people during crisis.

**Why I Love It:**
- The show balances intense horror with emotional depth — friendships and personal growth matter just as much as the scares.  
- It explores morality under pressure: who you become when survival is everything.  
- Great pacing and creative setpieces — every episode raises the stakes and keeps you on edge.

**Genre:** Horror, Thriller, Drama, Zombie Survival  
**Rating:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

**Favorite Moments (no spoilers):**
- The tense escape sequences that test the characters’ trust in each other.  
- Quiet scenes that reveal the emotional cost of surviving — these hit harder than the shocks.

**Watch Tip:** Pay attention to small character choices — they explain big changes later on.
